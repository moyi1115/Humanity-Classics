## Reading Framework

Each reading contains four core sections:

**Buddhism:** Sutra Text → Entering Prajna → Koan Highlights → Modern Application

**Taoism:** Original Text → Principle of Tao → Historical Mirror → Life Wisdom

**Confucianism:** Original Text → Words of Sages → Past-Present Dialogue → Self-Cultivation

**Western Philosophy:** Excerpts → Philosopher's Thought → Echo of Times → Reality Reflection

**Religious/Wisdom:** Excerpts → Words of Wisdom → Past-Present Dialogue → Contemporary Revelation

---

## Writing Style

- **Depth Over Length**: 1500-2000 words per chapter, quality over quantity
- **Direct to Core**: Sharp insights, no encyclopedic explanations
- **Cross-Civilizational**: Thought collisions between different classics and civilizations
- **Practice-Oriented**: Touching real pain points (anxiety, identity attachment, desire for control)

## Great Fish Mentor

An intellectual mentor based on 31 classic readings, helping you gain insights from classical wisdom in real-life dilemmas.

```
/wisdom
```

**Core Functions:**
- More than retrieval, it's understanding
- More than answers, it's inspiration
- More than solutions, it's growth

**Memory System**: Remembers your conversation history and growth, auto-cleans every 20 conversations.
